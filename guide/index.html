🏕️ The Camp

How to play, and how to beat every boss

▶️ Watch the 2-minute video first, then read on for all the tips.

There's a 2-minute video too: open the game and click “📖 How to play” on the start screen.

1Getting started

Single Player is just you. Multi Player lets you play with friends on other computers: one person makes a new world and gets a 6-digit code, and everyone else types it in to join. Friends share the camp's items and the Blob.

Then pick your camper. Each camper has their own saved game, so pick the same one next time and choose Resume to carry on, or Start Fresh to begin again.

3Exploring: items and adventures

Tents, trees, the Flying Car, the Moon… almost everything is a hotspot. Walk onto one and press Space to look. The pop-up shows:

  • 🧺 Here: click an item to put it in your backpack (it holds 10).
  • ✨ In your backpack: click one to use it here. That's an adventure!
  • 🔍 Find to use here: faded items. Point at one to see where it is.

You can't use an item where you found it, so carry things around the camp. There are 73 adventures to find, and each new one goes in your journal (J).

4Around camp

🎡 The prize wheel

Every new adventure gives you a spin. You can win coins, fight charms, or even a sword or armor. Each 3 adventures add a winning slot (up to 6 of 8), and a win takes 2 away.

💬 Friends and sidekicks

Walk up to a camper or critter and press T, then type anything. Ask “where is the sword?” (or any item) and they'll tell you exactly where it is.

Press 🤝 Be my sidekick! and they follow you through every door, and fight bosses with you.

🍳 The Camp Kitchen

Go through the pink door under the sink. Space grabs food, puts it on the stove, the cutting board or the plate. Put the right foods on the plate to make a recipe from the chalkboard (🥪 bread + cheese + lettuce), then eat it at the picnic table.

Food that doesn't match a recipe? Make up your own recipe and name it!

Desserts make you stronger for good: 🍪 $10 = +2 💪, 🧁 $20 = +5 💪, 🍰 $30 = +8 💪.

🏦 The Camp Bank

Penny the owl keeps your coins safe. Deposit them before a fight: if a boss beats you, it eats every coin in your backpack, but never the ones in the bank.

Shops only take coins from your backpack, so withdraw before you go shopping. There's a bank door on every camp, and it's the same bank everywhere.

5Fighting bosses

You need a 🗡️ sword. One is always lying somewhere on every camp (it glows), and friends will tell you where. Score the points first to win (10 against the Blob).

  • 💪 Strength sets how fast you move and how often your hits land. You lose 1 when you're hit and get it back slowly. Same for the boss.
  • Your sword wears out a little with every swing. A spare sword in your backpack is used automatically.
  • Win: a coin, and the boss gets a level tougher. Lose or run away: it eats your backpack's coins.
  • Charms from the wheel work by themselves: 🧪 +4 💪, 🫧 stops 2 hits, 🍯 saves you when you're weak, ✨ fixes your sword.

⚔️ How to win a fight

The boss always shouts before it attacks (“EAT!”). That's your cue: hold B to block, jump, or step back. Then swing while it's open. Don't just mash Space: the more you mash, the more it blocks, and after a block it hits back hard. A quick swing during its wind-up can knock it out of the attack, but armored wind-ups (it glows red-orange) just go CLANG.

8Top tips

  1. Bank first, fight second. Put your coins in the bank before any boss fight. Losing only costs what's in your backpack.
  2. Block on the shout. When the boss yells, hold B. Blocking well beats swinging fast.
  3. Always bring a sidekick. They swing too and their block guards you both. It's the easiest way to win more.
  4. Eat cake. Dessert strength is forever. 🍰 gives the most 💪 for your money. Strength 20 is where speed stops growing.
  5. Carry a spare sword. Pick up every 🗡️ you see. If yours wears out mid-fight, the spare jumps in.
  6. Do adventures between fights. Each one spins the wheel, and charms, coins and free gear add up.
  7. Start with Mini Mallow. It needs only 8 points and is easier, but only Mega Mallow wins open the way down.
  8. Gear up for the deep camps. Armor makes the Brick much easier (💎 Crystal nearly doubles your chances). For the Slime Brick, the ⭐ Star Sword + 🌟 Star Armor is the best team.
  9. Get the door codes first. Go to the Safe Zone on the Fourth Camp before anything else. The Slime Brick can catch you while you type!
  10. Scout with exploration mode. Press E to walk every camp with no bosses, and find things for later.

⏳ Play time: you get 20 minutes of walking and fighting (the ⏳ in the corner counts down; standing still, talking and shops don't count). Then it's a 1-hour break: go play outside! A grown-up can unlock more time with their code.
